Uma Sofia means "a Sofia" in portuguese (since there are so many of us). I approach my work as a playground where I sometimes design. With an experimental and multidisciplinary creative process, I cherish projects with unusual and anti-traditional concepts, where the line between design and personal identity is blurred — work that feels authentic, and a little unexpected.
